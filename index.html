Legal AI · Personal Injury · Lien Resolution
CaseZero
The connective intelligence layer plaintiff-side law doesn't have yet.

Personal injury law generates $53B in attorney fees annually. Tools exist to automate each stage in isolation — intake, medical processing, demand, negotiation, closeout, archive. But nobody has built the intelligence layer that connects all six, compounds learning across every case, and does what insurance companies can already do and plaintiff firms cannot: predict what a case is worth before a single demand letter is sent.

The wedge is lien resolution — a manual, legally complex, 4-hour process on every single settled case. I'm building it from the inside: I currently work at a personal injury law firm, processing cases, tracking liens, drafting demand letters, negotiating with adjusters every day. The dataset I'm building cannot be replicated by a competitor who hasn't lived it.

Healthcare AI · Prior Authorization
Sanctr
Prior auth appeals. 45 minutes → 3 seconds.

30% of orthopedic prior auth requests are denied on first submission. Writing each appeal takes 45 minutes — and 1 in 5 denied claims are never appealed at all. Sanctr generates a complete, clinically grounded appeal letter in under 3 seconds. Formatted, editable, ready to fax. HIPAA-eligible on AWS.
